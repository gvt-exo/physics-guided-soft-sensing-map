# Extended post-repair validation

Generated by `run_extended_validation.m`. No random shuffling is used. The March-June benchmark remains in `results/publication/`; this directory contains the independent extension.

## Data handling

- Process data end at `2026-09-29 00:00:00`; complete laboratory pairs end at `2026-09-29 00:00:00`.
- The repair gap is preserved from `2026-06-06 00:00:00` to `2026-07-07 00:00:00`; temporal filters reset at gaps over 5 minutes.
- The source workbook had 181 backward timestamp transitions and 181 duplicate timestamps. All channels were sorted together before filtering; no observation was deleted by sorting.
- Conductivity values above 30 are invalidated before smoothing. The gap-aware filter produced 2 independent segments.

## Exact validation splits

Intervals are left-closed and right-open. Rolling folds use 14 days for training and the next 7 days for testing. A calendar window enters the pooled comparison only when process-window alignment leaves at least 40 training and 10 common test laboratory pairs. A common test pair must be valid under both the frozen and adapted lag/window configurations so that their comparison uses identical targets.

## Configuration selection

The frozen configuration was selected on the last 14 pre-repair days. The adapted configuration was selected once on the first 14 post-repair days and then frozen. Both use a 9-day inner fit and 5-day inner validation. Coefficients are refit for every rolling training window.

| Strategy | Lag min | Window min | Mapping | Inner fit | Inner validation | Score |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---:|
| pre_repair_frozen | 0 | 60 | quadratic | 2026-05-23 00:00 to 2026-06-01 00:00 | 2026-06-01 00:00 to 2026-06-06 00:00 | 1.7951 |
| post_repair_adapted | 140 | 10 | pchip_spline | 2026-07-07 00:00 to 2026-07-16 00:00 | 2026-07-16 00:00 to 2026-07-21 00:00 | 5.3494 |

| Model | Training | Configuration |
|---|---|---|
| physics_guided_frozen | All eligible pre-repair observations | Physics-guided mapping selected before repair and then frozen |
| physics_guided_adapted | Previous 14 post-repair days | Physics-guided mapping selected on first post-repair training window; coefficients refit per fold |
| ridge_frozen | All eligible pre-repair observations | Ridge lambda selected chronologically within the pre-repair training set |
| ridge_adapted | Previous 14 post-repair days | Ridge lambda selected chronologically within each 14-day training set |
| physics_only | No fitted target mapping | P2O5 fixed at 52 wt%; unfitted acid-density prior |

## Interpretation and limitations

- `external_transfer_full` is a strict deployment-style test: neither fitting nor hyperparameter selection uses post-repair targets.
- `post_repair_comparison` compares frozen and adapted models on identical held-out laboratory rows. The adapted configuration is fixed after the first post-repair training window, preventing later-test leakage.
- Overlapping rolling training windows are intentional; test windows do not overlap. Pooled uncertainty is therefore descriptive and not an independent-sample confidence interval.
- The supplied historian export ends on 29 September although laboratory records continue into 1 October, so later laboratory values cannot be evaluated.
- Full per-split results (32 rows) and all predictions are retained in CSV files for audit.
